FAQS

Are the recipes on TypeNTaste free to access?

Yes, all recipes on TypeNTaste are completely free to browse and view. No subscription or payment is required to explore our recipe collection.

Can I search for recipes using ingredients I already have?

Yes! Simply type your ingredients into the ingredient input box and click "Generate Recipes." The system will filter recipes that match what you have.

What if I don't have all the ingredients listed in a recipe?

No problem! TypeNTaste suggests recipes based on what you already have. You can always skip or substitute missing ingredients. The recipe instructions will still guide you, and you can use our "Custom Recipe" feature in the Cook List to modify any dish to fit your pantry.
